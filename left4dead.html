Left 4 Dead 2

Fight for survival in Left 4 Dead 2, an intense cooperative zombie shooter where you and your teammates must escape hordes of infected. Use a variety of weapons, work together to survive deadly encounters, and make your way through dangerous cities and abandoned areas. Every moment is a fight for your life!
