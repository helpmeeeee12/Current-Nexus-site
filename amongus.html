Among us

Trust no one in Among Us, a multiplayer social deduction game where teamwork and deception collide. Work with your crewmates to complete tasks aboard a spaceship while impostors secretly sabotage the mission and eliminate players. Discuss suspicious behavior, gather clues, and vote out the impostors before it’s too late. Can you find the truth before everyone is eliminated?
