NEXUS

Company Background

Nexus is a proposed digital gaming platform founded by Vincent Baloco, Leonne Klein Balocod, Cacayan Kurt Ruzzel, and Aldrich Labitoria.

The idea of Nexus is similar to an App Store or Play Store, but it focuses on providing users with a platform where they can discover PC and mobile games in one place.

Nexus offers different types of games, including FPS, MOBA, RPG, survival horror, racing, strategy, sandbox, and battle royale games. Some of the games included in our catalog are Counter-Strike 2, VALORANT, League of Legends, Dota 2, Minecraft, Grand Theft Auto V, Red Dead Redemption 2, Genshin Impact, Mobile Legends, PUBG Mobile, Roblox, Among Us, and many more.

The main service of Nexus is to allow users to browse games, view their prices, genres, and descriptions, and choose games based on their interests. Our goal is to make finding and exploring games easier and more convenient for gamers.

For credibility and copyright, Nexus recognizes that the games in our catalog are owned by their respective developers and publishers. Nexus does not claim ownership of these games. If Nexus becomes a real gaming platform, we would obtain the proper licenses and permissions before distributing or selling any copyrighted games.

Overall, Nexus aims to become a convenient and organized platform that connects gamers with different PC and mobile games while respecting the rights and copyrights of game developers and publishers.
