Clive Barker's Hellraiser: Revival

Descend into a nightmare in Clive Barker's Hellraiser: Revival, a first-person survival horror game set in the world of the Hellraiser films. Play as Aidan, who must use a mysterious puzzle box to help his girlfriend Sunny escape from the Labyrinth. Survive the horrors of Hell, strike a bargain with Pinhead, and fight the cult that serves the Cenobites.

System Requirements

System Requirements
OS: Windows 10 64-bit (22H2 or later)
CPU: Ryzen 3 1200 / Core i5-4690
RAM: 8GB minimum
Storage: 50GB (SSD recommended)
GPU: GTX 1660 / RX Vega 64
