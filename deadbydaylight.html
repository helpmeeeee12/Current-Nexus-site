Dead by Daylight

Enter a terrifying world of survival in Dead by Daylight, an intense multiplayer horror game where four Survivors face off against one ruthless Killer. Play as a Survivor and work together to repair generators and escape, or become the Killer and hunt down everyone before they get away. Every match brings fear, suspense, and unpredictable challenges!
