Counter-Strike 2

Counter-Strike 2 is a competitive first-person shooter game developed by Valve. Players compete in teams as either Terrorists or Counter-Terrorists, completing objectives such as planting or defusing bombs and rescuing hostages. The game features tactical gameplay, realistic weapons, different maps, and team-based strategies. Players need good aim, communication, and teamwork to win each round.
