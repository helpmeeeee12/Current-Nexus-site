Outlast

Enter the terrifying halls of Mount Massive Asylum in Outlast, a survival horror game where danger lurks around every corner. As investigative journalist Miles Upshur, uncover the asylum’s dark secrets while hiding from terrifying enemies. You can’t fight back—your only weapons are your camera, courage, and ability to survive.
