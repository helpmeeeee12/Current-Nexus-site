League of Legends

League of Legends (LoL) is a free-to-play 5v5 multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game developed by Riot Games. Players choose from over 170 champions, each with unique abilities and playstyles. Teams work together to defeat the opposing team by destroying their Nexus. Players can choose different roles such as Assassin, Fighter, Mage, Marksman, Support, and Tank.
