Counter-Strike 2

Counter-Strike 2 is a competitive first-person shooter where players compete in teams. Teamwork, strategy, communication, and accuracy are important to win each round.

System Requirements

OS: Windows 10
RAM: 8 GB
CPU: Intel Core i5 750 or higher
Storage: 85 GB
DirectX: 9.0c
