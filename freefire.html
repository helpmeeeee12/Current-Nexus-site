Free Fire

Get ready for fast-paced survival action in Garena Free Fire, a Battle Royale game where 50 players fight to become the last one standing. Search for weapons, collect supplies, and use unique character abilities to outsmart your opponents. Team up with friends, survive intense firefights, and fight your way to victory!
