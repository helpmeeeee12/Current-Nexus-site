R.E.P.O.

Team up with friends in R.E.P.O., a thrilling co-op horror game where you explore terrifying locations to collect valuable objects. Use physics-based tools to carefully transport your loot while avoiding horrifying monsters. Work together, stay quiet, and escape with your treasures before everything goes wrong!

System Requirements

OS: Windows 10/11
RAM: 8GB minimum
Storage: 1GB
GPU: 4GB Video Memory
