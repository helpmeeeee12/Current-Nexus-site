Clash of clans

Build your own powerful village in Clash of Clans, a strategy game where planning and teamwork lead to victory. Train troops, upgrade defenses, join a clan, and attack enemy villages to collect resources. Work with your clanmates in epic Clan Wars and prove your skills as you grow your empire!
